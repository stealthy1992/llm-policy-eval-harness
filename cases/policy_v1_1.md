# Tulip & Teak: Shopping, Delivery and Returns Policy

Version 1.0. Effective 1 November 2026.

## 1. General
1.1 Tulip & Teak is an online store founded in Lahore in 2014. We sell apparel, home textiles and small home appliances, and we deliver within Pakistan only.
1.2 This policy applies to orders placed on or after 1 November 2026. Earlier orders follow the policy in force when they were placed.
1.3 If two sections of this policy conflict, the more specific section prevails.
1.4 In this policy, "days" means calendar days. "Working days" means Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## 2. Orders and payment
2.1 We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), debit or credit card, and bank transfer.
2.2 COD is available for orders up to PKR 25,000. COD orders above PKR 15,000 need a confirmation call and are cancelled if not confirmed within 24 hours.
2.3 Bank transfer orders are held for 48 hours awaiting proof of payment, then cancelled.
2.4 Prices include sales tax.

## 3. Delivery
3.1 Delivery takes 3 to 5 working days to Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad/Rawalpindi, and 5 to 8 working days to all other locations.
3.2 The delivery charge is PKR 250. Delivery is free on orders of PKR 4,000 or more, measured after discounts.
3.3 Orders placed after 6 pm are processed on the next working day.
3.4 If you are unavailable, the courier makes two delivery attempts. After two failed attempts the order is returned to us and cancelled.
3.5 Orders above PKR 10,000 are delivered with signature confirmation by the courier.
3.6 Orders above PKR 10,000 are delivered without signature confirmation.

## 4. Cancellations
4.1 You can cancel for free at any time before dispatch. We send an SMS when an order is dispatched.
4.2 After dispatch you cannot cancel, but you may refuse delivery. If you refuse delivery, the delivery charge and a PKR 250 return charge are not refunded.
4.3 Custom-sized curtains are cut to order and cannot be cancelled once cutting starts. Cutting starts 12 hours after order confirmation.

## 5. Returns
5.1 You may return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unused, unwashed and in its original packaging with tags.
5.2 Innerwear, socks and pillows cannot be returned.
5.3 Small appliances may be returned within 14 days of delivery if the box has never been opened.
5.4 Clearance items are final sale. They cannot be returned or exchanged, except for defects under Section 7.
5.5 Bundle offers (for example "3 towels for PKR 2,400") must be returned as a complete bundle.
5.6 A single item may be returned from a bundle within the return window. Its refund is its equal share of the bundle price.

## 6. Refunds
6.1 Refunds go to the original payment method. Card refunds arrive 7 to 10 working days after we receive the item.
6.2 For COD and bank transfer orders, you can choose a bank transfer to an account in your own name, or store credit.
6.3 If you choose store credit, we add a 5% bonus. Store credit expires after 6 months and cannot be exchanged for cash.
6.4 We inspect returned items within 3 working days of receiving them. Refunds are approved only after inspection.
6.5 The original delivery charge is not refunded, except when the return is our mistake (wrong or defective item).
6.6 Return pickup costs PKR 300, deducted from the refund, unless the return is our mistake.
6.7 Appliances returned with an opened box are subject to a 15% restocking fee.

## 7. Defects and wrong items
7.1 Report a defective or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp, with photos. Appliance reports also need an unboxing video.
7.2 For a verified problem you may choose a replacement or a full refund, including the delivery charge.
7.3 Appliances have a 6-month warranty from delivery. Warranty claims are repaired or replaced, not refunded. The warranty does not cover water damage, misuse or power surges.
7.4 Defects in apparel and textiles reported after 48 hours are not covered.

## 8. Exchanges
8.1 You can exchange an item once, for a different size or colour, within 10 days of delivery, subject to stock. The item must meet the conditions in 5.1.
8.2 If the new item costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is given as store credit, never as cash.
8.3 We collect the original item and deliver the replacement free of charge.

## 9. Sales and coupons
9.1 During named sale events (for example Eid Sale, 11.11), delivery is free on orders above PKR 3,000, and items bought in the event can be returned within 3 days of delivery only.
9.2 Only one coupon can be used per order. Coupons cannot be combined with sale-event prices. Coupon discounts apply to item prices, not delivery.
9.3 If part of a coupon order is returned, the refund equals the price paid for that item after its proportional share of the coupon discount.

## 10. Support and data
10.1 Support is available Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm Pakistan time, on WhatsApp and phone. We reply within 1 working day.
10.2 We use your name, phone number, address and order history only to fulfil and support orders. We do not sell personal data.
10.3 Disputes are governed by the laws of Pakistan.
